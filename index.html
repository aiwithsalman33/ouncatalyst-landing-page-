The learning experience

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Six essential career-readiness modules. One focused learning experience.

01

Core industry job search strategies

Discover and approach opportunities that match your degree and core domain.

  • Find roles related to your degree and core domain.
  • Explore company career pages, job portals, and industry platforms.
  • Identify relevant job titles, entry-level roles, and fresher opportunities.
  • Apply strategically instead of sending random applications.
02

ATS-friendly resume building

Communicate your strengths in a professional, role-focused resume.

  • Understand how ATS and recruiters screen resumes.
  • Build an ATS-friendly resume tailored to your target role.
  • Customize your resume to a job description.
  • Highlight skills, projects, internships, and achievements.
03

Free courses & skill certification

Find learning opportunities that strengthen your technical knowledge.

  • Discover reliable platforms with free courses in your domain.
  • Identify certifications relevant to your goals.
  • Select courses based on industry needs and skill gaps.
  • Build a learning roadmap and understand certificate options.
04

LinkedIn profile optimization

Build a professional presence and connect with industry professionals.

  • Reflect your core domain in your professional profile.
  • Optimize your headline, About section, skills, projects, and certifications.
  • Connect with recruiters, professionals, and alumni.
  • Approach people thoughtfully and build career connections.
05

Interview fundamentals & communication

Prepare for interviews and present yourself with greater confidence.

  • Understand common HR and core-domain questions.
  • Introduce yourself and explain your projects clearly.
  • Practice discussing strengths, weaknesses, and career goals.
  • Learn interview etiquette and professional conduct.
06

Mock interview & expert feedback

Practice in a simulated interview and receive constructive feedback.

  • Take part in a simulated interview with an industry professional.
  • Practice core technical and basic HR questions.
  • Receive feedback on understanding, communication, and confidence.
  • Identify improvements and plan your next preparation steps.
